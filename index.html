Weekly

Meal Prep

60g+ protein · Low carb · Mon–Fri

Sunday Prep

~90 min total · Follow in order

📦
0–5 min
Set up
Get out 5 lunch containers + 5 breakfast jars. Preheat oven to 200°C.
🔪
5–15 min
Prep everything first
Season your protein, chop all veg, measure spices. Get it all ready before anything hits the heat.
🔥
15–55 min
Cook proteins & roast veg
Everything into the oven or on the hob. Roast veg goes in at the same time. Run it all in parallel to save time.
❄
55–60 min
Cauliflower rice
If using fresh cauliflower rice, cook now. Or use frozen — microwave straight from the bag in 3 min, no prep needed.
📐
60–80 min
Portion lunches
Divide everything equally across 5 containers. Let them cool fully — 20–30 min — before sealing.
🫙
80–90 min
Breakfast jars
Fill 5 jars: oats, milk, yogurt, chia seeds, vanilla. Stir, seal, refrigerate. Done.

❄ Freezer reminder

Sunday: containers 1–3 → fridge, 4–5 → freezer
Wednesday night: move Thursday's to fridge
Thursday night: move Friday's to fridge
